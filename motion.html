Motion options

Ideas for the shirting landing page, each tied to one block. Everything moves with opacity or transform only, so nothing shifts layout, and nothing waits on motion to show content. Hover, scroll and replay the samples. Turn on "Preview reduced motion" to see the fallback.

Tokens: 150 micro · 300 UI · 700 reveal · settle cubic-bezier(0.2, 0, 0, 1) · fade cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 0.2, 1)

M1 · Intro line on load

Check LCP

Eyebrow and line fade from 0 to 1 over 500ms, settle easing, no delay and no movement. The page's first screen in v4 and v5. If the intro line is the LCP element, the fade can push LCP back by its duration, so this one needs a check before it ships.

M2 · Story images on scroll

My pick

Each image fades in and rises 16px over 700ms, settle easing, once, when 15% is in view. The second image follows 100ms later. Transform only, so the grid box never moves. Reduced motion: images are simply there.

M3 · Lazy images fade in when decoded

My pick

Every loading="lazy" image fades in over 300ms once it decodes, on the gray-20 placeholder that's already there. Cached images skip the fade. Replay simulates a slow load. Reduced motion: images appear when ready.

M6 · Carousel arrow step

Arrows scroll the row over 600ms with fade easing, replacing the browser's default smooth scroll (its speed varies by browser). Swipe and trackpad stay native. Reduced motion: the row jumps, as today.

M7 · Details tabs: slower cross-fade, body follows

My pick

Image cross-fade goes from 400ms ease to 600ms fade easing, so the two photos overlap evenly. The tab's line fades in over 250ms, 100ms after the switch. Click a tab to compare. Reduced motion: instant swap, as today.

M8 · Details tabs: slow drift while rotating

Bolder

On top of M7, the active photo drifts from 1.00 to 1.03 over its 6 seconds, linear, and stops where it is when someone pauses or picks a tab. It's covered by the existing pause control (C14). Reduced motion: no drift, and rotation never starts.

M9 · Editorial band settles in

When a quarter of the band is in view, the photo eases from 1.06 to 1.00 over 1200ms, once, and the quote fades up 8px 200ms later. Not parallax: it doesn't track the scroll. Reduced motion: static.
